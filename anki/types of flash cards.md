there are several card types lets start with the simplest:

- **basic**:in basic you have only front and back text.When questioned you will be shown the front and will have to guess the back as the answer of the question![[Pasted image 20241113045044.png|lp right]]
**NOTE** to pass from front text to back text instead of clicking we press 'tab'.

**reverse card** in anki allow you to create 2 cards in the same time.How?

- by writing the front and the back of card you create 2 card 1 that has front as front and back as back and another that has front as back and back as front which means that the question of this card can also be the answer and the same is true for the opposite.


**cloze deletion** allows you ro create fill the gap type of questions you can turn text into fill the gap by using this syntax : `{{c1::text::hint}}` c1 represents the order of close deletion.Each close deletion will create its separate card text represents the ansewr to be revealed and hint will be showed as text in front as a hint to give general idea of what fill the gap is about.

**image occlusion**:

lets you select an image and hide parts of it to turn it into a guessing game and each hidden part will turn into a card (one image conclusion could create multiple cards).There are 2 modes for image occlusion:

1. **Hide one guess one**:lets assume you selected a map image and hid all the names within that image. when you use that card **only** one part of the image ill be hidden for you to guess while the others are still visible to give you a hint.
2. **Hide all guess one**: this is the opposite and more challenging version of above. You are trying to guess a single **highlighted** hidden part while the others part are hidden as well (no helps or tips).
