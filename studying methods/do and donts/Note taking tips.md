1. **Visual Notes:**  The most effective note-taking strategy involves creating visual representations like diagrams, flowcharts, or mind maps. 

2. **Active Processing**:Note taking is not a replacement for thinking, we should understand the information before writing it that can be by engaging with the material, asking questions, and form connections between concepts.

3. **Beyond Note Rereading**:  reading note over and over will not improve our memory Instead we should use active recall apps like anki.Notes are generally used to just understand information rather than memorize them

4. **Note Evolution and Linear vs. Non-linear**:  We should always revisit our notes and update them when necessary.  Linear notes follow a structured format, while non-linear notes utilize visual elements to showcase relationships between concepts.

2. **QEC Framework for Non-Technical Courses**: "QEC" which stands for question evidence and conclusion is a studying method used for non technical courses like history and law.It emphasize the importance of understanding information as questions try to find evidence to answer the question and come to a conclusion based on the evidence
	- **NOTE**: it is possible to have more than a signal conclusion for a question and each of those conclusions must have evidence supporting them.

4. **PSA Framework for Technical Courses:** For technical courses like math, science, and economics, we should use the PSA technique which stands for problem steps and answer. It emphasize the importance of understanding information by trying to solve a problem related to it and recording the steps we did to find the solution
	- **NOTE**:It is important to attach detailed explanation for each step we used to find the solution.Also it is possible to come to the same answer using different steps/methods

5. **Create summary cheat sheet of notes**:This is helpful because it forces us to form connections and try to show our understanding of the subject in as fewer words as possible(active learning = active engagement).It also saves us time when we want to get a quick reference from our notes.
6. **Understand before writing**: jumping from tasks to task takes time because we are switching from one thinking method to another. This results in inefficient use of time making the learning process slower.To prevent this we should fully understand before writing our notes. By fully engaging with the material, asking questions, and making connections between concepts, we become task-independent as we then have a good understanding of the subject matter.
7. **taking notes while watching a video**: its always recommended to watch a video and in the middle of it take note about what we  did and didnt understood and the key points of they video that make the process of watching the video engaging and memorable.
