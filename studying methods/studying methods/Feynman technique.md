The feynman technique is the process of trying to explain a complex topic in an easy to understand (format/wording) even to a 12 yo

Here are a couple of tips to improve this technique:

- don't try the feynman technique until you fully understand a topic, otherwise you will be wasting your time trying to explain something you're not sure about
- after we successfully explain the topic in simple terms we can now memorize them with flash cards
- Not every topic can be used with the feynman technique especially if its already simple
- avoid complexity, the simpler the better
- put in effort to simplify the topic so YOU can understand it ,as long as the explanation doesn't seem simple enough to understand in the future it means its not good enough
