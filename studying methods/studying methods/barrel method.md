The barrel method is used to identify our weaknesses related to our studying methods.Its called the barrel method because you can fill a broken barrel even if only **one** plank is broken this shows that all study skills are needed to be a good learner and we cant ignore one of them. This methods recommends reviewing our studying skills/methods every month and try to identify our weaknesses and seek to improve them.It also mentions 5 studying skills to look for which are:

1. **Deep processing:** the ability to:![[Pasted image 20241116145845.png|lp right|500x300]]
	- understand complex subjects easily
	- form strong memories
	- create connections between idea
	- solve hard problems
2. **Self awareness:** the ability to:![[Pasted image 20241116150351.png|lp right|500x300]]
	- know how we are learning (the methods we use and how we think)
	- adjust our learning technique and improve them for the better
	- admit that our system is not perfect and a change is necessary for us to improve
3. **Mindset:** the ability to:![[Pasted image 20241116150750.png|lp right|500x300]]
	- respond to stress situations
	- recognize and improve from our mistakes
	- deal with challenges
4. **Retrieval:** The ability to:![[Pasted image 20241116151159.png|lp right|500x300]]
	- recall and revisit what we learned
	- test our self and see our level of understanding
5. **Self management:** the ability to:![[Pasted image 20241116151443.png|lp right|500x300]]
	- manage our time and tasks
	- maintain our focus through out the day
	- avoid distractions
	- use our time efficiently and wisely
