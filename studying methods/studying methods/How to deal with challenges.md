## How to view challenges

- Challenges are endless waves that get harder and harder with each wave

## How to prepare for challenges

- instead of finishing a challenge , preparing for an another and repeat forever (which will leave us burnt out) we can :
	- first get a clear idea of what our next challenges are so we can prepare for them before and get a great start
	- instead for preparing for each challenge we can prepare once for multiple challenges (like improving our learning technique as preparation for studying)
	- The first preparation will be the hardest after we have created a good learning system preparing for other challenges wont feel as hard or exhausting
