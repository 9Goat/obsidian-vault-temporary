there are 3 layers of thinking that we have to go through for any topic for maximum efficiency **IN ORDER** the more layers we go through the easier it gets, those 3 layers are :

- **logical understanding**: creating connections between concepts to form a clear picture of how they interact and relate to one another, the best way to this is by :
	- creating a list of the all of the concepts and keywords in our study
	- try to group all of the keywords and concepts by possible logical connections or similarities
	- This is extremely helpful when note taking especially
- **in depth** after we get the full picture of how all the main points and concepts relate to each other we are now still missing the specific details that complete each individual concept that is why we should do full research on every concept in depth (this section will take the majority of our time when studying like %70)
- **memorizing the important** a mistake that a lot of students do is create flashcards for concepts the moment they learn it which basically skips layer 1 and 2 and leaves us memorizing things we dont need/fully understand,we only need to memorize what is **relevant** to us after going both layers (by using anki preferably)
