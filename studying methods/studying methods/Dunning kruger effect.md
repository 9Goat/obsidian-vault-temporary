
this effects explains the steps on how we learn something new here is how it goes:

1. **Mountains stupid**: This is what happens when there is a huge increase in our confindence but a small increase in our actual skills. This can happen because of our over confidence about our skills which leads to lack of awarness of our actual skill leave (we dont know what we dont know) this prevents skill improvement because we thing we *understood it all*
	- To avoid being stuck in mountain stupid is good to actual be aware of our skill by using the **premortem analysis** technique:
		- This technique evolves around imagining the worst scenario/negative outcome and trying to explain to our self why it happened that (potential reasons) helps us identify our skill gap 
2. **Valley of despair**: This is what happens when we have a huge decrease in our confidence in our actual skills. Despite learning something new and gaining knowledge this happens after we discover a skill gap in ourselves and start to lose hope in ourselves But its important to not give up and continuously challenge ourselves and work on improving our skills gradually.
3. **Slope of Enlightenment**:This is what happens we acknowledge our skill gaps and try to improve from our mistakes This leads to a rapid growth equal to a high increase of confidence
