## How to use layering

to layer a subject we need to divide it into separate parts depending on the difficulty.But how?

- we can scan through the entire subject and determine visually what looks hard to understand and what it not
- OR use ai to do that for us! Just supply the ai with you learning resources (documents) and ask it to layer it by difficulty
- divide our studies into multiple runs with each run covering an increasing difficulty level
