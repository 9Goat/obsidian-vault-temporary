cramming is a study technique often employed by students when faced with an exam in a short period of time,Even though its highly discouraged there are some methods to make it better:

- imagine yourself as the professor what will the exam will look like? what topics will you question about? what type of questions will we use?
- only focus on subjects that either will cover a large percent of the exam or are easier to learn by others we need to learns topics depending on difficulty/cover percentage
- we need to admit that no matter what we do we have to blame ourselves for getting in this situation and we shouldnt expect a high result but realistic approach will help us limit our losses
- when cramming when should first get a clear picture and understanding of the subject by looking at the key points rather than the details by using different methods such as [[reading backwards]] and shuffling between resources searching for similar keypoints , when we are confident with our understanding then we can dive into details
- Another great strategy is to create a study plan depending on the subjects/materials you have in that certain period of time here is how it should be:
	- usually we want to split our study evenly between days (not in terms of subjects numbers but in terms of 'how long will this take me to complete'), sometimes in days where we have more free time its okay to dedicate more study for that day
	- we should our study into 2 categorize:
		- **Must do**: what we must learn in that period of time
		- **if times allows to**: should only do after the must do , involves tasks that will help us understand the subject a lot like doing extra practice papers or going more depth in a subject 
- the last day before the exams should we only do recapping and practice papers(never try to learn something new in the last day)
- the morning of the exam should be only a quick 1-2 hours of reviewing nothing more
- we should focus first to the thing we are the weakest at rather than opposite, if we finish our weak subjects and become good at it that will improve our result rather than increasing what we already are good at. This make us efficient with out time (80% of the results come from 20% of the effort)

##  cramming early


cramming early means trying to study the majority of the subjects either at the start of the semester or even before it .This approach makes our brain get the big picture and understanding of the subjects we are going to study in which we can then add to it day by day.This is helpful because:

- our brain gets prior knowledge to the subjects which will later on make adding information to it much easier and our brain wont forget it easily
- cramming early will put out brain under pressure trying to understand a lot of information in a short time expect that it is not before the exams which means there is no risk in cramming early
