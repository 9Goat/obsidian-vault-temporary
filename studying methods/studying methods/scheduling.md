To improve our productivity its important to schedule or tasks in certain times here is how:

- **Prioritizing**: select tasks to do that your future self will be the most thankful for and avoid things that done matter until later
- **Generous time blocks**: Its always better to over estimate the length of a tasks  rather than under estimating it. Why?
	- that is because we can always add more tasks for the time we have left after finishing but not the opposite, if we under estimate that means we have to modify the entire schedule
- **Assume the worst**: there are always scenarios where we wont get everything done and we have to accept that and keep that in mind (especially if we set unrealistic goals)
- **Personal preference**: I personally prefer using tasks management rather than time management because time management can be very unpredictable
	- This is how i like to set my tasks :
		- **Task name**
		- **Duration**
		- **Date** (decide whether it should be in the daily/weekly/monthly/yearly tasks)
		- **Starting time**
		- **Place**

## minimized scheduling

instead of using complex time schedule systems some peoples prefer a simpler alternative that being :

- few tasks in a day 1-5 with no (time requirement,place,starting time ,,etc) just the name of the task with an open check box
- if we dont complete all of the tasks in that day we just move it to the next day and don't add more tasks to it
 the point of this method is to decrease stress and make management easy and not over whelming
