## Problem

Our brain has a processing capacity (cognitive resources) that it cant exceed we learn new things quickly our brain becomes overload with information it cant process which will result in us forgetting it and performing poorly.

## Solution

Instead of rushing things and trying to learn as many things as possible at a fast pace, we need to accept that learning was meant to be slow.here are a couple of tips :

- Try to focus on 1 thing at a time, rather than spreading your attention across multiple subjects. This will help your brain process information more efficiently and retain the knowledge longer.
- we should divide our learning into 2 sections **theory** and **practice**.
- for every hour of theory we should do 5 hours of practice(may change depending on the complexity of the subject).
- the goal of this method is to turn information into habits (things that we do/understand automatically without having to thing about them) by practicing them often and moving them into out long term memory 
- when something becomes a habit it free s up cognitive resources, allowing us to devote attention to new information and skills, rather than constantly trying to recall and re-learn previously acquired knowledge.
- to tell if we are building a habit or not we should track our progress over time and notice if we can perform the task faster , more efficiently and automatically.
