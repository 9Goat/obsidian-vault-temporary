
## Health and motivation / focusing

- [[health is more important]] we should always prioritize our health which means 8 hours of sleep daily from 19-->3 and drinking water regularly
- [[listening to music]] listening to lofi music only during tasks that doesn't require intense focusing
- [[5-minute rule]] we trick our brain by saying "we will only do this for 5 min!" to make hard tasks seem less bad to start
- using the pomodoro technique for better focusing capacity (**settings:** 25 minute work sessions followed by 5 min break repeated for 3 times with a long break of 10 after 3 sessions)

## Studying sessions

- [[as many resources]] gather as many resources before studying something
- [[benefits of note taking]] using note taking for every subject for active learning (don't just copy the resources try to instead write it how you understood it in your own words) in **obsidian note taking app**
- [[learning by doing]] for any and everything new we learn must **AT LEAST** spend 5x the time it took us to learn it in order to fully master it
- [[Using AI for studying]] we should use ai to enhance our studies (Google Notebook,perplexity,chatgpt,cloud,gemini and text-to-speech ai reader)
- [[Feynman technique]] we try to explain what we **fully** learned in simple words because explaining things acts an active way of learning
- [[Anki tips]] Utilize Anki, a spaced repetition flashcard software, for efficient memorization of crucial information.
