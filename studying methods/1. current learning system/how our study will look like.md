
1. we first start by collecting all of the resources we need (dont collect more than necessary to avoid wasting time,sometimes a single resource is enough)
2. basically the goal is to create a full on a structured table of content on the topics we will be learning, then once we are fully finished then we can start taking actual notes 
3. when we learn a new thing, we close the resource and try to explain it in our own words and write bullet points of what we just understood (brain dump notes), after that we try to correct what we got wrong in our explanation
4. we practice we what have just learned (finishing a part of the note) until fully mastered
5. after doing a bit of practice to strength our understanding we can then create flashcards of them

# Things to avoid

- just copying and posting notes instead of brain dumping, the problem with that is that we are not actively thinking about what we are learning and instead passively copying and pasting the notes
- make few flash cards and not many, when we are done writing a note about a section, we should try to not create a flash card about every specific detail instead try to group them, for examples we should make a flash card about a specific features and then a specific card about 3 of its components, not each card for each component, this helps us reduce the time we have to create and learn from flash cards
- remember to spend the majority of our time practicing rather than learning, since this is where most of our experience and understanding will come from
