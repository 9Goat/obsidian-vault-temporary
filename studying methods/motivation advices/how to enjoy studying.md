## by using dopamine

dopamine is a resource that makes our brain feel happier more focused and existed but how do we use it to make studying enjoyably instead of boring:

**TIPS:**

- meditation
- the first and last 30-1h should never include [[procastination]] activities its always recommended to start your day with work to be productive for the rest of the day
- Turn studying into a game by using TODO checklists here is how 
	- turn any task into a check *but not a single check list* break it down into smaller easy to do tasks that you can complete and check one by one, this will give you a sense of accomplishment and increase your dopamine levels as you see yourself completing tasks and getting closer to your goal. Another way to improve this method is by giving yourself special rewards after finishing some tasks like a snack or a short break here is an example
		- read 5 pages - check 
		- write 3 paragraphs- check
		- create a flash- check 5 minutes break
