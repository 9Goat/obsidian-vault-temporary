procrastination means being consistently distracted by other things while performing something important like studying this can be frustrating .This happens because our brain prefers doing simple and shorts tasks for immediate reward rather than doing actual good work for rewards.

## Are we addicted to procrastination

Follow these simple steps to know:

- every time we get distracted by something while doing tasks we should write what we got distracted by in full details
- remove all distraction that we noted
- if we still find ourselves getting distracted, or wanting to get distracted or feeling tired all the time not wanting to do anything or feeling weird and unusual as if our brain is seeking for something to distract itself by that means we are addicted

# Solutions

Over coming any addiction in general is challenging here are some steps to help us through:

- remove all distraction
- use meditation
