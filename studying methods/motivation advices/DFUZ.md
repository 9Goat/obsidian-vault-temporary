1. "D" stands for Distinguish, emphasizing the separation of feelings (like tiredness or laziness) from thoughts and actions.

2. .  "F" is for Fake, acting as if one were not tired or lazy, even if the feelings persist. 

3.  "U" represents Uptime, focusing on gradually increasing the duration of time spent in the defused state and repeat that process daily. 

4. "Z" stands for Zone, which emphasizes creating a distraction-free environment to minimize the need for consistent high motivation. A great way to create distraction free zone is every time we get distracted by something we should write it in a not and try to avoid it
