While listening to music to is relaxing it does have its pros and cons depending on the music and the task we are performing:

## Types of musics

For maximum productivity its better to choose relaxing music that doesnt have lyrics and has a consistent, calming rhythm. This type of music can help improve concentration and reduce mental distractions, creating an environment conducive to focused work. like lofi

## depending on the task

there are 2 types of tasks that we can perform:

**Requires thinking** : for tasks that requires intense focus and thinking its better to avoid all types of distractions including music . While it can be relaxing music can shift our attention away from focusing on the study resources like watching educational youtube videos that requires real time focus

**repetitive tasks**: On the other hand, for repetitive tasks that require less focusing , listening to music can have a positive impact on our mentality and increase our productivity ad reduce our stress and keep use entertained without feeling burnt out.
