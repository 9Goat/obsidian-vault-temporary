Its really hard to maintain focus during studying because of many distractions here are a couple of tips to help us stay focused:

- **1 task commitment**: instead of switching from 1 tasks to an another thinking we are *multi-tasking* instead we should focus on every task individually to avoid forgetting where we left the tasks on when we switched.
	- if we end up having to switch tasks maybe because it takes too long to complete its better to leave it in the middle rather than the end of it
- **Pomodoro technique**: This involves round turning our working time into this format to help our brain reset, get the rest it deservers and maintain our focus:
	- **session rounds** you can divide your working time into sessions rounds **preferably 3** where each session round will be less or equal to 60minutes  each and that 60 minutes will be divided into :
		- **80-75% work**
		- **20-25% rest**
		- **example:** 45 work / 15 minute break or 50 work /10 minute break or 25 work / minute break,,,,etc
	- after we complete 3 or more session rounds we should reward ourselves with a long break which is approximately 2-3 times the short break  
	- here are some things to do when taking break:
		- do not spend your break in the work place.Why? that is because standing in the same place for too long feels tiring and could make you feel unrefreshed and lead to distraction. Instead, change your environment
		- do other activities that are not related to our phone or computer ,,,etc
		- instead do physical relaxing activities like meditation,closing our eyes for few moments and think/talk to ourselves,,,etc
- our brains feels focused the most at the morning which makes it the best time to study
- sometimes we might feel exhausted and unable to focus because of dehydration that is why we should drink water regularly
