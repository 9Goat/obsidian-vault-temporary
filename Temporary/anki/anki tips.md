## When to review cards
 Every day when we have free time

## When to create cards

after finishing our notes we can create flash card (either by ourselves or by using AI to speed the process)

**Note**: Not every information have to be turned into a flash card 

## Anki overload

**anki overload**: when we get too many flashcards to review in a single day that it makes us feel unmotivated

### How to avoid it

- **Daily review and consistency**: if we review our flashcard everyday we wont suffer from anki overload
- **splitting time**: we dont have to review our  flash cards in 1 time we can split them between after noon,morning, free time ,,,etc
- **not everything is worth a flashcard**: avoid the very obvious stuff that everyone knows and only focus on things that we might forget in the future to reduce the number of flash cards we create
- **use the load balancer anki addon**
