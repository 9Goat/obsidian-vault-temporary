basically a for loop just like in programming.  to exit out a loop, you can use the "break" statement, similar to how it's used in programming languages. the condition of the loop can be one of the following:

- a specified number (for example loop(10) will execute 10 times)
- loop(variable in varaible) just like regular programming
- loop will loop infinitely
- loop(min,max) for example (loop(5,10) will loop a minimum of 5 times and a maximum of 10 times)
