An **n-ary association** is a relationship that links **more than two classes** simultaneously. This is different from the common binary (two-way) associations you usually see.

- **Central Diamond (Losange):** An n-ary association is represented by a central **diamond shape** connected by solid lines to all participating classes.
- **Association Class:** The central diamond can optionally contain or be linked to an **Association Class** (via a dashed line) if the relationship itself needs to hold attributes or operations.

# How to write [[multiplicity]] in n-aire 

The multiplicity on the line next to Class A specifies how many instances of **A** are linked to one specific combination of instances from **all the other classes** involved (B, C, D, etc.).
