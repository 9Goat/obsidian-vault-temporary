
# phrases explanation

## matters arising

In a meeting, **matters arising** refers to a specific time on the **agenda** used to talk about things from the _previous_ meeting.

##  any other businesses 

In a meeting, the phrase **"any other business"** (often abbreviated as **AOB**) refers to the final part of the **agenda**. This is the time when people can bring up small topics or questions that were not officially listed on the plan.

## office gossip

**Office gossip** refers to casual conversations between coworkers about the private lives of other people in the office or secret information about the company.

## inhouse magazine

An **in-house magazine** is a magazine that is written, designed, and published by a company specifically for its own employees or its own customers.

# words explanations

## agenda

an **agenda** is a list of items or topics to be discussed. It is like a "to-do list" for a meeting to make sure everything stays organized and finished on time.

##  chairperson

A **chairperson** (often just called **"the chair"**) is the person who is officially in charge of a meeting, a committee, or an organization.

Think of them as the "referee" or the "captain" of a business discussion.

## minutes

In a business meeting, **minutes** has nothing to do with time or clocks. Instead, it refers to the **official written record** of what happened during the meeting.

# types of meetings

|**Type of Meeting**|**Short Meaning**|
|---|---|
|**Meeting to maintain contact**|A quick check-in to keep people connected.|
|**Brainstorming meeting**|A session to think up new and "zany" ideas.|
|**Decision-making meeting**|A meeting where the group picks a final plan.|
|**Discussion meeting**|A talk to share opinions on a specific topic.|
|**Annual General Meeting**|A big yearly meeting for all members or owners.|
|**Information meeting**|A meeting where a leader gives facts to the staff.|
|**Negotiation**|A meeting where two sides try to reach an agreement.|
|**Planning meeting**|A session to organize future tasks and dates.|
|**Problem-solving meeting**|A meeting to find a way to fix a specific trouble.|
