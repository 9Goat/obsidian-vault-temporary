# phrases explanation

## Pay promptly

**Pay promptly** means to make a payment **quickly, on time, and without any unnecessary delay**.

## firm order

A **firm order** is a binding, final agreement to buy a specific quantity of goods or services at an agreed-upon price, with fixed delivery dates.

## Letter of credit

A **Letter of Credit** (often abbreviated as **LC**) is a formal financial document issued by a bank that guarantees a buyer's payment to a seller will be received on time and for the correct amount.

It is primarily used in international trade, where the buyer (importer) and seller (exporter) do not know each other well, live in different countries, and need a reliable middleman to ensure a secure transaction.

# words explanations

## Dumping

**Dumping** is an international trade practice where a company exports a product to another country at a price that is significantly **lower** than what it normally charges in its home market—and often lower than the actual cost to manufacture it.

## Quotas

**Quotas** are government-imposed trade restrictions that limit the physical quantity of a specific good that can be imported or exported during a set period (usually a year).

## Tariffs

**Tariffs** are taxes or duties that a government places on goods imported from other countries.

## Subsidise

**Subsidise** (or **subsidize**) means when a government or an organization gives financial aid, cash, or tax breaks to a business or industry.

The goal of a subsidy is to lower the cost of production for those companies. This keeps the prices of essential goods or services low and affordable for the public, while helping local businesses stay competitive against foreign rivals.

## Deregulation

**Deregulation** is the process where a government removes or reduces its rules, laws, and restrictions on a specific industry.

## Customs

**Customs** government department responsible for collecting taxes on goods

## Concessions

It is something valuable that you choose to give up, lower, or grant to the other side to move the deal forward.

**Example**: During the collective bargaining meetings, the tech company made a **concession** by offering an extra five days of paid annual leave, convincing the developers to sign the new contract.

## Commissions

**commissions** refers to the extra money you earn on top of your base salary whenever you successfully close a deal or sell a product.

## Dispatch

**to dispatch** means to officially send off a shipment or package to a customer
