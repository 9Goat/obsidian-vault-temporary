
# Phrases explanations

## build up a range

"build up a range" refers to the process of gradually increasing the variety or number of products, services, or ideas offered by a person or a company.

**Example**: To remain competitive against larger rivals, the startup decided to **build up a range** of eco-friendly household products

## swarm a market

To **swamp a market** means to flood a specific market with such a large quantity of a product that the supply far exceeds the demand.

When a company "swamps" a market, they are usually trying to overwhelm their competitors by making their own product so available (and often so cheap) that it is the only thing consumers see or buy.

## dreamt up (might remove later)

This phrase is more **imaginative and whimsical**. It suggests that the idea was born from a spark of inspiration, fantasy, or even a "crazy" vision.

## inventor's fair

An **inventor's fair** (also known as an **invention fair** or **innovation expo**) is a specialized event where people who have **thought up** or **dreamt up** new products gather to show them to the public, the media, and potential investors.





#  Words explanations

##  Zany

In this context, **zany** describes something that is **unconventional, and funny in a bizarre way.

If an advertising campaign is "zany," it isn't just a standard commercial; it is likely weird, energetic, and perhaps a bit silly to grab people's attention.

## patent

A **patent** is a legal right granted by a government to an inventor. It gives the inventor the exclusive right to stop others from making, using, or selling their invention for a specific period of time

##  exhibit

To **exhibit** means to publicly display a product, an idea, or a quality so that others can see it.
