# phrases explanation

## Stock market

The **stock market** is basically a public marketplace where people buy and sell pieces of company ownership (**equity**).

## to plunge/plummet

**To plunge** means to fall, drop, or crash down suddenly and very fast.

- **Note**: the opposite of plummet is soar

## Interest rate

**Interest rates** represent the cost of borrowing money or the reward for saving it.

It is expressed as a percentage of the total amount borrowed or saved:

- **For Borrowers:** The extra fee you pay a bank to take out a loan.
- **For Savers:** The extra money the bank pays you to keep your cash in an account.

## to level off 

**To level off** means to stop rising or falling and become steady, flat, or stable.

# words explanations

##  Turnover

**turnover** is simply the total amount of money a business brings in from selling its goods or services during a specific period. It is another word for **total sales** or **gross revenue**.
## Recession

A **recession** is a significant decline in economic activity that lasts for months or even years.

During a recession, the economy shrinks, businesses make fewer sales, companies cut spending, and many people lose their jobs.

## Forecast

A **forecast** is a prediction or estimate of what will happen in the future based on past data and current trends.

## Equity

**Equity** means **ownership** in a company.

When you own equity, you own a percentage of the business. If the company succeeds and grows in value, your equity becomes worth more money.

## Dividend

A **dividend** is a portion of a company's profits paid out directly to its shareholders.

## Bureaucracy

**Bureaucracy** is a system of managing an organization or government through strict, official rules, complex procedures, and a clear hierarchy of authority.
