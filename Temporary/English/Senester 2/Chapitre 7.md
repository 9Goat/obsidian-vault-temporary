# phrases explanation

## Price fixing

**Price fixing** is an illegal agreement between rival companies to sell a product or service at the exact same price.

Instead of competing against each other to offer the best price to customers, they work together secretly to keep prices high and maximize their profits.


## Money laundering

**Money laundering** is the illegal process of making "dirty" money (cash earned from crimes like drug trafficking or fraud) look "clean."

Criminals run the illegal cash through a legitimate, legal business (like a restaurant, a car wash, or a fake company) so that when the money comes out, it looks like normal, legal profit that they can safely spend or put in a bank.

## Insider trading

**Insider trading** is the illegal act of buying or selling a company's stock (shares) using private, secret information that the public does not know yet.

Because the person has "inside" information, they know whether the stock price will go up or down, giving them an unfair financial advantage over regular investors.

## Tax evasion

**Tax evasion** is the illegal act of deliberately not paying the taxes you owe.

## Blew the whistle

**Blew the whistle** (or to "blow the whistle") means to publicly report illegal, unethical, or dangerous activities happening inside an organization.

# words explanations

## Espionage

**industrial espionage** or **corporate espionage**, it means a company steals a competitor's secret data, product designs, or code to gain an unfair advantage.

Instead of researching or building their own tech, they just steal it.

## Counterfeit

**Counterfeit** means an illegal, fake copy of a real product. It is made to look exactly like the original item to trick people into buying it, usually because the brand name is famous or expensive.

## Sacked

**Sacked** is an informal, everyday term that means being **fired** or dismissed from your job.
