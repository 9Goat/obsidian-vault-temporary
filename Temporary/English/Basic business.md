# words explanations


- **Market share** is the percentage of total sales within a specific industry or market that a particular company or product controls.
- The word **subsidiaries** means **smaller companies or businesses that are owned and controlled by the main, larger company** being discussed.
- A **shareholder** (or **stockholder**) is a person, company, or institution that **owns shares** of stock (the price of a stock is called *share price*) in a company.
- **Shares** represent a small piece of ownership.
- The shareholder has a right to a portion of the company's **profits** (called **dividends**) and, in some cases, the right to **vote** on major company decisions.
- A **receptionist** is the **person** whose job is to work at the reception (place). They are the "face" of an organization and are usually the first point of contact for external visitors and callers.
- in business, a **union** (or **trade union**) is an **organized group of employees** who join together to use their collective strength to negotiate with the employer
- **After-sales** refers to all the **support and services a company provides to a customer _after_ they have bought a product or service.**
-  **Senior:** Someone with **many years of experience** in a role. They have a high level of expertise, work more independently, and often **mentor, lead, or supervise** junior staff.
- **Junior:** Someone with **less experience** in a role, typically just starting out. They require **more supervision and training** and focus on learning and carrying out core tasks.
- Clerical work is a type of **white collar job** that focuses on the **day-to-day administrative tasks** that keep an office running smoothly.

# phrases explanation

i took the minutes of the meeting

- It means **I wrote down the official notes (or record) of what was discussed and decided at the meeting.**

i fixed an appointment with a client

- "I arranged a specific time and date to meet with someone."

a branch of bank

- A **branch of a bank** (also known as a banking center or financial center) is a physical, retail location that is a secondary office of a larger banking corporation.

**Cashing a Cheque/bank note:** Turning a paper cheque into spendable cash or deposited money.

An **assembly line** is a manufacturing process where a product moves along a line (often a conveyor belt) from one workstation to the next
