
# 🎨 Digital Art Master Map

## 1. Workspace & Setup

_Before you draw, you need to prepare your canvas._

- **[[Canva size]]** – How to set the dimensions and resolution of your digital workspace.
- **[[Grid]]** – Using overlays to help with alignment, scale, and precision.
- **[[symmetry]]** – Mirrors your strokes to create perfectly balanced designs effortlessly.

## 2. Basic Drawing Tools

_The essential tools for making marks._

- **[[pencil]]** – The primary tool for sharp lines and pixel-perfect precision.
- **[[brush tool properties]]** – Adjusting size, hardness, and texture to change how your strokes look.
- **[[line]]** – A tool for creating perfectly straight edges between two points.
- **[[Rectangles]]** – Quick geometric tool for creating perfect squares and boxes.
- **[[Circles]]** – Quick geometric tool for creating perfect ellipses and rounds.
- **[[Eraser]]** – Removes pigment or pixels to correct mistakes or carve shapes.

## 3. Editing & Refining

_Tools to help you tweak what you’ve already drawn._

- **[[selection tools]]** – Isolating specific parts of your drawing to move or edit them.
- **[[move tool]]** – Repositioning selected elements or entire layers across the canvas.
- **[[Eye dropper]]** – Samples a color directly from your canvas for exact matching.
- **[[Bucket fill]]** – Fills an enclosed area or specific color range with a solid color instantly.

## 4. Layer Management

_Organizing your work so you can edit parts of your art independently._

- **[[adding a new layer]]** – How to create fresh transparent sheets to keep your work organized.
- **[[Layer properties]]** – Adjusting blending modes, opacity, and locking settings.
- **[[changing between layers]]** – Navigating the layer stack to ensure you're drawing in the right place.
- **[[group  layers]]** – Organizing multiple layers into folders for better project management.
- **[[flip options]]** – Mirroring layers to check for anatomical or perspective errors.
- **[[Out line tool]]** – Automatically creating a border or silhouette around layer content.
- **[[flatten]]** – Merging multiple layers into one single image for finalization.

## 5. Color Mastery

_Finalizing the mood and palette of your piece._

- **[[Color panel options]]** – Overview of the interface used to pick hues and values.
- **[[Editing a color]]** – How to tweak existing colors to get the perfect shade.
- **[[changing and adding colors]]** – Saving your favorite shades to a palette for consistent use.
