## Important

a general rule of thumb for all shapes is that if the area of the shape is flat then we will use a single color as shade otherwise if the area of the shape is curved or round, we will use a gradual transition of shades to show the curvature, with the shade getting progressively darker or lighter depending on the direction of the curve and the light source.

![[image-254.png]]

# Cylinder

a cyclinde is a combination of flat and round areas meaning:

- the top surface will be shaded with a single color
- the bottom surface will be  shaded with a gradian color  depending on the direction of the light source
