# Free tier vs paid tier

Unlike Knowt—which restricts how many files you can convert with AI each month—**Thea offers unlimited AI file uploads and study kit generation on its free tier**, only applying daily question caps to its adaptive quizzing and practice tests. 

According to the [Thea+ overview](https://www.theastudy.com/thea-plus), here is how the free tier compares to the paid **Thea+** plan across its core features:

## Thea: Free vs. Paid Tier (Thea+)

|Feature|Free Tier|Paid Tier (Thea+)|
|---|---|---|
|**Study Kit Creation & Uploads**|**Unlimited** uploads across 90+ file types (PDFs, PowerPoints, handwritten notes, YouTube links)|**Unlimited** uploads with priority processing (no wait times during peak hours)|
|**AI Flashcards**|**Unlimited** auto-generated flashcards from your uploaded materials with built-in spaced repetition|**Unlimited** auto-generated flashcards|
|**Study Guides & Summaries**|**Unlimited** AI-generated breakdowns and summaries from any uploaded source|**Unlimited** AI study guides and summaries|
|**Smart Study (Adaptive Q&A)**|Included with a **daily cap** on the number of questions you can answer|**Unlimited** Smart Study questions with no daily caps|
|**Practice Tests**|Included with a **daily cap** on the number of test questions you can answer|**Unlimited** full-length practice tests and exam simulations|
|**Games**|**Unlimited** access to interactive review games|**Unlimited** access to all review games|
|**Audio Podcasts & Extra Tools**|Basic/limited access (varies by tool; core text/card features remain unlocked)|**Full access** to premium study tools (such as AI podcasts) and zero wait times|

## Key Takeaway vs. Knowt

- **Thea's Model:** You can upload as many lecture slides, PDFs, or videos as you want to generate notes, study guides, and flashcards for free. The only wall you might hit on the free plan is during heavy cramming sessions when answering large volumes of **Smart Study** or **Practice Test** questions in a single day. [[1](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=study.thea.www.twa), [2](https://www.theastudy.com/post/what-is-thea-plus)]
- **Knowt's Model:** You can answer unlimited practice test questions for free, but converting your files into notes or flashcards with AI is restricted to **1 file per month**. [[1](https://notibo.net/blog/knowt-alternative)]
