# Free vs paid

Based on the **Knowt Help Center**, **core study, browsing, and manual creation tools are completely free**, while the paid **Knowt Ultra** plan removes monthly AI generation caps and unlocks audio/voice features across the tools shown in your screenshot.

## Learn & Create Features

|Feature|Free Tier|Paid Tier (Knowt Ultra)|
|---|---|---|
|**Exam Predictor & Plan**|Limited (shares the monthly AI generation/summary cap; 1 free full-length mock exam)|Unlimited custom study plans, file uploads, and AI exam predictions|
|**Summarize file(s)**|**1 AI summary per month** across PDFs, images, videos, or PowerPoints|**Unlimited AI summaries** for all file types|
|**Live Record Class**|**1 live recording summary per month** (shares your 1/month AI summary limit)|**Unlimited AI lecture recording** and automatic note/flashcard generation|
|**Notebook**|Unlimited manual note creation, storage, and live collaboration; AI tools inside notes are capped|Unlimited notes plus unlimited AI writing/summarizing tools inside your notebook|
|**Flashcards**|Unlimited manual creation, Quizlet imports, and all study modes (Learn, Spaced Repetition, Match)|Unlimited creation and study modes, plus unlimited AI flashcard generation from files|
|**Podcasts**|**Not included** (Ultra-only feature)|**Full access** to the AI Podcast generator for any notes or flashcards|
|**Practice Test**|**Unlimited** practice tests generated from your existing flashcards/notes|**Unlimited** practice tests, plus unlimited AI-generated tests from uploaded files|
|**Play**|**Unlimited** access to interactive study games (such as Match and Knowt Play) on your sets|**Unlimited** access to all interactive games, ad-free|
|**Call with Kai**|Available on your own existing flashcard/note sets|**Full access** to the 1:1 AI voice tutor across all uploaded files and materials|
