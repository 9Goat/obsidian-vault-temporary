# Features

**Yes, Gemini Guided Learning supports interactive quizzes, flashcards, step-by-step worked examples, diagrams, and videos** rather than just plain text. 

Interactive Study Tools & Formats

- **Interactive Quizzes.** You can ask Guided Learning to quiz you on a topic or uploaded notes. Quizzes open in an interactive panel (Canvas) where you can click answers, request hints, and view a final score breakdown highlighting your strengths and focus areas. 
- **Interactive Flashcards.** You can generate flip-style flashcards from any conversation or study file. The flashcard interface includes shuffle mode, swipe navigation, hints, and text-to-speech audio playback. 
- **Worked Examples & Socratic Dialogue.** Instead of giving you the final answer right away, Guided Learning walks through problems step by step, prompts you with open-ended questions, and adjusts its feedback based on your responses.]
- **Images, Diagrams & YouTube Videos.** Responses automatically pull in relevant visual aids, explanatory diagrams, and embedded YouTube videos to help illustrate complex concepts (provided your Gemini Apps Activity setting is turned on).
- **Study Guides & Practice Tests.** You can turn your session or uploaded documents into structured study guides or timed practice tests (including full-length formats for exams like the SAT, JEE Main, and NEET UG).

How to Trigger Them

While inside a **Guided Learning** chat, you can ask for these directly using simple prompts—or upload your own notes/PDFs first using **Add Files**: 

- _"Quiz me on what we just covered."_
- _"Turn these notes into interactive flashcards."_
- _"Walk me through an example problem step by step."_
- _"Show me a diagram or video explaining this concept."_

_(Note: Interactive quizzes, flashcards, and YouTube embeds may have age or administrator restrictions on certain Google Workspace for Education accounts.)_
