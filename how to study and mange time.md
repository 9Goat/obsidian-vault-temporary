# how to get myself to study

- try to stay outside the bed, you cant study well when im in a comfortable position
- try to make the studying session fun
- start with something very small and achievable to begin with
- actually use the pomodoro technique
- write what im going to do for the day (for the beginning only plan for the day not next weeks or months unless i get consistent at it)

# How to study efficiently

- the best way to learn it to learn by doing not by memorising
- create exercise and exams for myself using ai to practice all the questions
- dont cram everything in one session, small consistent sessions is all need
- first get the big picture then jump into details, How?
	- first ask ai like claude to summarize everything into a well formatted document
	- also ask ai for all the key concepts that i need to learn and in what order
	- once i understand what im trying to learn is all about, then i can move forward to learning in details and practicing what i learn
- try to take notes in a well formatted way with many examples and excalidraw drawings (yes making effort in writing notes makes it easier to remmember and understand especially if it is made with illustrations and drawings and examples)
- to learn something in details i like to use ai projects and feed it with all my resources i have and then ask it to first write a plan/table of content that it has to follow in its explanations and at the end of each explanations it asks me questions to test my understanding o the subject
- to test if i understand something very well try to explain it to others (inside your head)
